{\footnotesize\ttfamily uint8\+\_\+t encode\+\_\+animatronic\+\_\+data (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}\item[{\mbox{\hyperlink{animatronic_8h_a32f0f4417c28e3d435e7c6f2c4ee9dc4}{Animatronic\+Name}}}]{name,  }\item[{\mbox{\hyperlink{game__constants_8h_a731c885d938c53ac3bedf5e2f6226850}{Camera\+ID}}}]{position }\end{DoxyParamCaption})}



Encodes the animatronic\textquotesingle{}s name and position (camera) into a single byte of data to be sent through the serial port. 

The encoding uses the 2 most significant bits to represent the animatronic\textquotesingle{}s name, the next 4 bits to represent the camera number, and the 2 least significant bits to represent the camera letter (A, B or C). 
\begin{DoxyParams}{Parameters}
{\em name} & The name of the animatronic to be encoded. \\
\hline
{\em position} & The camera position of the animatronic to be encoded, which includes both the camera number and letter. \\
\hline
\end{DoxyParams}
\begin{DoxyReturn}{Returns}
The encoded byte of data representing the animatronic\textquotesingle{}s name and position. 
\end{DoxyReturn}
