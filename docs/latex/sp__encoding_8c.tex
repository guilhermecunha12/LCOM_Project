Encodes the animatronic\textquotesingle{}s name and position (camera) into a single byte of data to be sent through the serial port. 

The encoding uses the 2 most significant bits to represent the animatronic\textquotesingle{}s name, the next 4 bits to represent the camera number, and the 2 least significant bits to represent the camera letter (A, B or C).
